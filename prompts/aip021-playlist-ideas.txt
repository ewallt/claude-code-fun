AIP021 — Playlist Ideas

You are going to generate specific video topic ideas for a playlist, distributed across underrepresented structural categories and filtered to avoid duplicates.

Ask the user for the following before proceeding:
1. Which playlist or subject area (e.g. History Potpourri, World War Two, Big Ideas)
2. How many ideas they want
3. Their existing video titles — ask them to paste the list, or say "none yet" if starting fresh

---

THE 13 STRUCTURAL CATEGORIES

1. Coincidences
2. Small causes with big effects
3. Near-misses
4. Wrong turns
5. Accidental discoveries
6. The thing invented twice
7. The expert consensus that was completely wrong
8. The solution that created a bigger problem
9. The fake that became real
10. The prediction that came true
11. The decision that almost went the other way
12. The discovery hiding in plain sight
13. The wrong person in the right place

---

STEP 1 — IDENTIFY UNDERREPRESENTED CATEGORIES

Read the existing video titles and reason about which of the 13 categories each represents. Pick 2-3 categories that appear least (or not at all) in the existing list. These are the categories to generate from. Do not show this analysis unless asked.

---

STEP 2 — GENERATE AND DISTRIBUTE

Distribute the requested number of ideas across the chosen categories. For example, 8 ideas across 3 categories: 3 + 3 + 2.

For each idea:
- Fit the structural category
- Fit the playlist scope and tone
- Be narrow and specific — one incident, one place, one chain of events
- Have a clear surprising angle or irony

---

STEP 3 — FILTER FOR DUPLICATES

Remove any idea that closely matches an existing video. A close match means a viewer who saw the existing video would feel they already got this one.

If fewer ideas survive filtering than requested, note the gap and offer to generate from another category.

---

STEP 4 — PRESENT

Present as a numbered list grouped by category. Lead each idea with a proposed YouTube-style title, followed by one sentence on the angle.

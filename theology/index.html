Better
than that

However good you think God is, He's better than that.

That conviction runs through everything here. Jesus said, He who has seen Me has seen the Father (John 14:9). No one has seen God at any time. His only Son, who knew Him best, has shown us what He is really like (John 1:18, paraphrased). As we keep looking at God through Jesus, He keeps turning out to be better than we thought.
